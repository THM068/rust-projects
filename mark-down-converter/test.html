Chloroquine is generally considered safe and effective for preventing and treating malaria when used as prescribed in regions where malaria parasites are still sensitive to it. However, there are important considerations:

  • Effectiveness: In many parts of the world, malaria parasites (especially Plasmodium falciparum) have developed resistance to chloroquine, making it ineffective.
  • Safety: When taken at the correct dosage, side effects are usually mild (e.g., nausea, headache, itching). Serious side effects are rare but can include vision or heart problems, especially with long-term use or overdose.
  • Prescription only: Chloroquine should only be taken under medical supervision, with proper diagnosis and dosing based on local resistance patterns.

Before taking chloroquine, consult a healthcare provider to confirm:

  • Whether it’s appropriate for the region you’re in
  • The correct dosage for your age, weight, and health status
  • Possible interactions with other medications
